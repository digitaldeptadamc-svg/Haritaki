Department of Dravyaguna
Haritaki
Botanical Name :

  • Terminalia chebula Retz

    • Family Name :

  • Combreetaceae

    • Local Name :

  • Alalekayi

    • Vernacular Name :

  • Hindi –Harad

  • English – Chebulic myrobalan

    • Synonyms :

  • Abhaya

  • Pathya


    • Habitat :

  • Haritaki tree is found all over India especially in forests of North India,Central provinces and South India


    • External Morphology :

  • Leaves : 7 – 20 cm long and 4 – 8 cm wide,glabrous or nearly so when mature,not clustered

  • Flowers : All hermaphrodite,4 mm,across,sessile,dull white or yellow with an offensive smell

  • Fruit : Pendulous drupe,2 – 4 cm long,ellipsoid or obovoid from a broad base,glabrous


    • Useful Parts :

  • Phala (Fruit)


    • RasadiPanchaka :

  • Rasa –Kasaya, Madhura Amla,katu Tikta(Pancarasa)

  • Guna –Laghu Ruksa

  • Vipaka - Madhura

  • Virya –Usna


    • Pharmalogical Actions :

  • Antimicrobial

  • Antifungal


    • Therapeutic Administration:

  • Parinama : Haritaki taken with jiggery and sugar relieves Parinama sula

  • Vatavyadhi: Regular usage of Haritaki with cow’s urine cures Vatavyadhi


    • Dose

  • Curna (Powder ): 1 g (Rasayanartha)

  • Curna (Powder): 6 g(Sodhanartha)

  • Kwatha(Decoction):30 -50 ml


    • Formulation:

  • Haritakyadi curna

  • Haritakyadi kwatha
